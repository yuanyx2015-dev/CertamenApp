## 1.1 — July 11, 2026

Current live version.

A major update to CertamenPrep — how you study for Certamen has been rebuilt from the ground up.

**NEW LOOK**
- Fresh app icon and name: CertamenPrep
- Four tabs: Home, Challenge, Review, and Practice
- Swipe left or right to switch tabs quickly

**CHALLENGE MODE** — your main path to rank up
- Questions appear letter-by-letter, like a real Certamen toss-up
- Buzz in once the full clue appears, then answer before time runs out
- Work through sets of 10–50 questions and climb 11 Roman ranks, from Miles to Legatus Legionis
- Track mastered questions, progress in your current rank, and what still needs review on Home
- Finish a set for a clear summary: mastered, correct, and wrong
- Build a daily streak by playing Challenge Mode each day
- Earn today’s streak and celebrate with confetti and a Roman-themed message

**PRACTICE MODE** — stress-free studying
- Six categories: Mythology, History, Language, Literature, Culture & Life, and Living Latin
- Same typewriter questions and buzz-in timing as Challenge Mode
- Misses here don’t add to Review — great for warming up or trying a new topic
- Hold the star on a correct answer to mark it mastered
- New end-of-session screen shows how many you mastered and how many you missed

**REVIEW MODE** — fix your mistakes
- Miss a question in Challenge Mode? It goes to Review automatically
- Work through everything you got wrong, category by category
- Master a question in Review to remove it from your wrong list
- Clearer on-screen tips explain how Review works

**HOME SCREEN**
- See your rank, progress bar, mastered count, remaining questions, review count, and streak at a glance
- Streak counter uses your device’s local time — midnight to midnight, where you are
- “Start your Daily Challenge!” jumps straight into Challenge Mode
- Sign in with Google or Apple to save progress; continue as Guest to try Practice
- Log out or delete your account from Home when you need to

**POLISH & FIXES**
- Smoother buttons and clearer labels throughout
- More accurate wrong-question counts on Home (Practice no longer mixed in)
- Set length stays at the size you picked
- Optional rating prompt after your first completed set, then at mastered milestones

Good luck climbing the ranks. Vale!

## 1.0.1 — May 7, 2026

**AI Explanations + Follow-Up Tutoring**
Tap “Explain with AI” on any review question to get a clear, context-aware explanation of why the correct answer is right. Not satisfied? Ask up to 2 follow-up questions per day and get conversational answers from your personal Latin tutor.

**Smarter and Faster**
Explanations are cached — so popular questions load instantly without burning your daily quota. The AI uses the best available model automatically, with graceful fallback so you’re never left waiting.

**Bug Fixes and Reliability**
General stability improvements and smoother error handling throughout the app.
